# SMB Connector

## Configuration

```env
CONSMB_BIND_ADDR=<Connector bind address>
CONSMB_BIND_PORT=<Connector bind port>
CONSMB_SMB_SHARE_ADDR=<SMB Share Host address>
CONSMB_SMB_SHARE_PORT=<SMB Share port>
CONSMB_SMB_SHARE_SERVICE_USER=<SMB share service user>
CONSMB_SMB_SHARE_SERVICE_PASS=<SMB Share service user password>
CONSMB_SMB_SERVICE_MOUNT_PATH=<Where to mount the share as the service user>
CONSMB_SMB_USER_MOUNT_PATH=<Where to mount the share as a requesting user>
CONSMB_SYSTEM_NAME=<SMB Connector name>
CONSMB_SMB_SHARE_NAME=<Name of the SMB share>
```
